We launched a new spike detection feature for all users on plans including Anomaly Detection (Enterprise and custom plans only). With spike detection, elmah.io automatically monitors your applications and store an error in your log when an error spike is identified. Unlike the impact increased email that we had for a couple of years, the new spike detection is superior in the following ways:

- Based on machine learning.
- Logs spikes as errors which make them visible on your dashboards.
- Send out emails using a custom email template including a chart and common errors included in the spike.
- Send spike messages through integrations like Slack and Teams.

If you currently have both the *New Error* and *Impact Increased* emails enabled, we recommend you to turn off the Impact Increased email on your [profile](https://app.elmah.io/profile/#emails).

We hope that you will enjoy the new feature. As always, reach out for any questions.
