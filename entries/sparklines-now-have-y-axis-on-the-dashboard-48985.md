A request that we've received often, is some additional info on the sparkline charts on the dashboard. The sparkline was originally meant as a small gimmick, but we realized that it served as a good quick overview. Without numbers on the y-axis, it wasn't possible to see if a spike were caused by 5 or 5,000 errors, though.

Today, we released a small improvement, showing the maximum number of errors visible on the graph, when the user hover each log:
