We just released the first chapter in our new email bounce feature. We've had a manual blacklist of bounced emails in the past, but now we've automated that part. From today, bounced emails are automatically added to the blacklist. If your email bounce, it will show up on your profile:

![Showing a bounced email](/images/89478/451-f33746ea3f1c5b269c761410aa70050beace8f4a.png)

The New Error and Daily Digest emails have been ported to the new blacklist and the rest of the emails will follow over the next couple of weeks. Blacklisting bouncing emails is the best way we can keep a good email reputation and make sure that the emails we send, end up in your inbox.
