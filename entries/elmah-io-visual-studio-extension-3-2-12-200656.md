There's a new version of the [elmah.io extension for Visual Studio](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=elmahio.elmahio). The new version contains a range of bug fixes and some internal restructuring to support Visual Studio 2022. The Visual Studio Marketplace doesn't yet support extensions with both 2019 and 2022 support in the same listing why we have uploaded a preview of the 2022 version here: https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=elmahio.elmahiobrowser2022. Feel free to play around with it if you are experimenting with Visual Studio 2022 (we are and we like what we have seen so far).
