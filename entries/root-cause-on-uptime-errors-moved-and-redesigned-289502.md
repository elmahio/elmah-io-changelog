The *Root Cause* tab previously shown in the extended error details, has been moved to the error details shown when extending an error on the *Search* page. While moving the content, we also took the chance to give it an overhaul, making the information more available and consistent with the [new Heartbeat view](https://changelog.elmah.io/root-cause-view-with-heartbeat-checks-287553) released last month:
