Support for the gpt-4.1 and gpt-4.1-mini models has been added to the ChatGPT app.

- Organizations on Business and Business+ plans bring their own OpenAI API key and choose any available OpenAI model.
- Enterprise customers can choose between both OpenAI-hosted and elmah.io-hosted models.

> OpenAI GPT-4 is now deprecated. If your app is currently using OpenAI GPT-4, it will automatically be upgraded to OpenAI GPT-4.1 in the coming weeks.
