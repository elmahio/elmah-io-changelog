The cookie popup, that you have probably already interacted with on elmah.io, can now show up on app.elmah.io as well. If you have accepted cookies on elmah.io, you should not see the popup again on app.elmah.io.

The new popup introduces a better grouping of cookies and improved options for disabling different cookies. If you want to include the Intercom support widget (the message icon in the lower right corner) you will need to accept cookies of type *Preferences*. The *Statistics* group contains only Google Analytics and we don't use any marketing cookies like Google Ads tracking, remarketing, or similar.

By navigating to the *Details* tab in the cookie popup, you can see a complete list of cookies used across the websites:
