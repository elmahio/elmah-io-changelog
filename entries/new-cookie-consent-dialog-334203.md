We have replaced our cookie consent dialog with a more streamlined solution designed to improve transparency and reduce configuration overhead. We've clarified the descriptions for all active cookies and condensed the required user choices by eliminating non-essential categories. As part of our ongoing effort to minimize the data footprint on elmah.io, we have successfully reduced our requirements to necessary and preference cookies only, completely removing all tracking scripts. You will see the updated dialog upon your next visit to elmah.io or app.elmah.io.
