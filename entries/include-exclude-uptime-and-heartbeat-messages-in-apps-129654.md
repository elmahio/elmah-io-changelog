All of our notification apps now provide toggles for Uptime Monitoring and Heartbeats (Heartbeats currently in closed beta). Using the new toggles, you can configure the app to include or exclude messages logged through either Uptime Monitoring or Heartbeats when sending messages to Slack, Microsoft Teams, etc.:
