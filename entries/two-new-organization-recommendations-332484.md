We released [Organization recommendations](https://changelog.elmah.io/organization-recommendations-332428) yesterday, and today we are launching the first two new recommendation types:

- A recommendation to notify you when your organization has users with bounced emails.
- A recommendation to notify you when your organization has users with unverified emails.
