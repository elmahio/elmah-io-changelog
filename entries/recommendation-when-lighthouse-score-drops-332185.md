There's a new recommendation created when the Lighthouse check is enabled on an uptime check, and the scores drop below a certain value. The Impact follows those of Lighthouse itself, where a score below 50 creates a High impact recommendation and a score between 50 and 89 creates a Medium impact recommendation.
