There's a new integration with Pipedream. For anyone not knowing Pipedream, it's a service similar to Zapier and IFTTT that lets you integrate online systems without any coding needed. Currently, there's a single integration point, being a trigger that initiates when a new error is logged in an elmah.io log.

Check out how to set it up in the documentation: [Integrate with Pipedream](https://docs.elmah.io/integrate-elmah-io-with-pipedream/).
