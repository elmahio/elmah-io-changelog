Admittedly a few years overdue. But you can now delete your account directly from the elmah.io UI. As always, user accounts are automatically deleted when not part of an organization and inactive for a longer period. This new change introduces an *Actions* tab on your profile to manually delete your account:

![Delete user account from the UI](/images/288454/555-a68094257755ea3a2d62417741d2f60cb8f7739c.png)

[Documentation](https://docs.elmah.io/how-to-delete-my-account/) has been added explaining the requirements needed to successfully delete an account.
