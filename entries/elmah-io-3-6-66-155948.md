We released a new version ([3.6.66](https://www.nuget.org/packages/elmah.io/3.6.66)) of the `Elmah.Io`, `Elmah.Io.Mvc`, `Elmah.Io.WebApi`, and `Elmah.Io.AspNet` packages. The new version includes the recent version of `Elmah.Io.Client` as well as enrichers for common ASP.NET related exception types.
