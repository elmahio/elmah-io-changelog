There's a new favorites feature available in the logs menu and on the dashboard. One or more logs can now be marked as favorites. You either mark a log as a favorite by editing it on the dashboard or by clicking the favorite icon next to the existing icons when hovering the log in the left menu. The menu and entire dashboard can be filtered to only show favorite logs by clicking the Favorite icon at the top.
