There's a new *Creativity Level* dropdown on the ChatGPT app. With this, you can select different levels of creativity generated by ChatGPT when asking for error resolutions:

![image.png](/images/305050/890-ed1fda344d053b5d445b038fc267e4a6a2f7d305.png)

Conservative for focused and predictable outputs, Default for balanced responses, or Explorative for imaginative and diverse ideas.

In addition, the number of input tokens needed for elmah.io to "speak" with ChatGPT has been reduced by 60%.
