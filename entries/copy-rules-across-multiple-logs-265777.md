A new copy button on rules will let you easily copy and paste rules across multiple logs or even within the same log. When copied, the rule details will be available on the clipboard. To paste the rule, simply click <kbd>CTRL</kbd>+<kbd>P</kbd>. We have chosen <kbd>CTRL</kbd>+<kbd>P</kbd> over <kbd>CTRL</kbd>+<kbd>V</kbd> to avoid disabling native paste features within this page.
