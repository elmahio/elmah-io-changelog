There's a new prerelease of the `Elmah.Io.Umbraco` package [available on NuGet](https://www.nuget.org/packages/Elmah.Io.Umbraco/5.0.19-pre). The new v5 package brings elmah.io logging to Umbraco 10 and newer, by installing all of the needed packages as well as providing the Health Check notifier known from the old package to Umbraco 10, Umbraco Cloud, and Umbraco Uno. The [documentation](https://docs.elmah.io/logging-to-elmah-io-from-umbraco/) has been updated to reflect the latest changes.
