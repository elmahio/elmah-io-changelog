If you are not already subscribed to the elmah.io newsletter, it is easier than ever. The new toggle on your [Profile](https://app.elmah.io/profile/), lets you subscribe (or unsubscribe) in two mouse clicks. We send out our newsletter every 2-3 months, filled with content about elmah.io, ASP.NET Core, Azure, and much more.
