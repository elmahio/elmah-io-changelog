The new batch actions feature (available in the Daily Digest Email) has been extended with a new button: Ignore in the future:

![Ignore in the future](/images/70379/124-6499f17039574df37cd41e2f23efaddf9380cf83.png)

When clicking this button, new instances of this error, won't be logged moving forward. As always when ignoring based on the uniqueness hash available in elmah.io, a new release of your software, will resume logging of an error. Check out [Deployment Tracking](https://elmah.io/features/deploymenttracking/) for more details.
