We improved the Detailed Usage Report of both messages and emails, available when clicking the information icon next to *Messages* and *Emails* on the organization settings page. Using a pie chart and log names over IDs you get a much better overview of where your log messages are spent each month.

![Detailed usage report.png](/images/166877/525-621f82a85dc3a604e2c3cc4ec8e1a2a99ea718b8.png)

In addition, you can now click a log and jump directly to this month's usage on the log overview.
