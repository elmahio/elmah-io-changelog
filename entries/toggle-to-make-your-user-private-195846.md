There's a new toggle named **MAKE PRIVATE** on your [profile](https://app.elmah.io/profile/#profile). As explained above the toggle, enabling that will exclude your user from showing up in the search result on the *Add user* page. Once your user is added to your organization on elmah.io, you typically don't need others to be able to see your name and profile photo in the search result.
