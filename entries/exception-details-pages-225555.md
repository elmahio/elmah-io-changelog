We have launched details pages for a range of commonly logged exceptions on elmah.io. For an example, check out the [System.NullReferenceException](https://elmah.io/exceptions/System.NullReferenceException/) page. While the official pages on the Microsoft Documentation site are fine, they are very much focused on properties and methods. On top of that, there's often not much help to avoid each exception. With the new exception details pages, we are trying out a different approach, by combining exception statistics from Stack Overflow, YouTube videos, and code examples for helping to avoid throwing exceptions in the first place. We hope that you will like these pages and we are currently evaluating how to build this into the elmah.io app.
