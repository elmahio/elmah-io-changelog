We just published one of the most requested features: Custom environments. Previously, there were 4 hardcodes environment names that you could group your logs by: `Production`, `Staging`, `Test`, and `Development`. With the new Environment admin on your organization settings page, you can create the environments you want and assign custom names:

![Custom environments](/images/158642/662-207b9cbfa48273519fac3029dca23eb78ad40bc1.png)

The environments are grouped in the same order as on the admin page on Dashboard and on the left menu. You can drag and drop environment names on the admin page to reorder.
