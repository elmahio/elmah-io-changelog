There's a new bot suggestion message shown on log messages suspected as generated by an automated bot/spider/crawler. The suggestion is being generated by machine learning and is available on plans with the Machine Learning feature.

![Bot suggestion](/images/178280/855-f67d692fd961898b027c1040b679fdee98f24d67.png)

You can mark the error group as generated by a bot or not directly from the message or you can hide the message once (by clicking the close icon) or permanently.

In the future, you will be able to execute actions based on the new flag. Like ignoring similar messages going forward.
