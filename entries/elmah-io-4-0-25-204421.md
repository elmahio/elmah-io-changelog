We released a new version ([4.0.25](https://www.nuget.org/packages/elmah.io/4.0.25)) of the `Elmah.Io` package. The release is built on top of the newest version of the new `Elmah.Io.Client` v4 package moving from AutoRest to NSwag.

Upgrading from `3.x` to `4.x` may require changes in your code. Check out [Upgrade elmah.io from v3 to v4](https://docs.elmah.io/upgrade-elmah-io-from-v3-to-v4/) for details.
