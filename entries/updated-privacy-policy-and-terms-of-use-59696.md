We have been working hard to be GDPR compliant by May 25. Today, we have published our revised [privacy policy](http://elmah.io/legal/privacy-policy/) and a while back, we combined our terms and conditions and terms of use into a new [terms of use](https://elmah.io/legal/terms-of-use/) document.

As part of GDPR, we will ask you to review and accept the new privacy policy and terms of use on your next login. We actually tried to write something that doesn't require an attorney to understand. We have also tried to by 100% transparent about the data we collect and how it is handled.

Let us know if you have any questions for the updated terms.
