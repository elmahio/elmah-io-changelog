We've noticed an increasing number of email addresses registered on elmah.io bouncing. Because we are charged with sending bounced emails, starting January 2024, bounced emails will be included in the monthly email quota.

Make sure to check if your email is causing a bounce on the [profile](https://app.elmah.io/profile/#profile):
