Users logging in with a username/password can now enable two-factor authentication on the [profile](https://app.elmah.io/profile/) page:

![Two-factor authentication](/images/125762/764-19d38b46a5d405b0a3a4e557ede3d1e4a27aa066.png)

You can use either Google Authenticator or Authy. For users using a social provider, all of the options provide two-factor authentication natively.
