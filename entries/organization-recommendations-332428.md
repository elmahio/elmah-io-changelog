Recommendations now cover your elmah.io organization. Access the new *Recommendations* tab by clicking the *Overview* icon next to the organization name on either the dashboard or the left menu.

This new view focuses on organization-wide insights rather than individual logs. This will include recommendations regarding the general state of your organization, security or configuration issues, and aggregated data analysis across multiple logs. We will continue to expand these insights to identify trends and potential problems that span your entire account.

While the list is currently empty, we are deploying the first two organization recommendation types within the next 24 hours. To stay updated, click the *Subscribe* button in the top-right corner of the view to receive email notifications when new organization-level recommendations are generated.
