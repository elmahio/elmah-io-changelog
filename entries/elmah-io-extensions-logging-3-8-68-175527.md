We released a new version ([3.8.68](https://www.nuget.org/packages/Elmah.Io.Extensions.Logging/3.8.68)) of the `Elmah.Io.Extensions.Logging` and `Elmah.Io.AspNetCore.ExtensionsLogging` packages. The new version includes a small fix of an exception sometimes thrown by ASP.NET Core when accessing Form variables.
