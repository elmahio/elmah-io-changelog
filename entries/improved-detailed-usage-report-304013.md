The *Detailed Usage Report* has been heavily improved. elmah.io will now include a chart showing how many messages were processed daily. This will help you track down on which day or days where most of your monthly quota was used. The new view also lets you click a single log to see metrics specific to that log.

The report is available from the *Subscriptions* tab on the Organization Settings page.
