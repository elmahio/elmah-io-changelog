We're busy migrating to Stripe, but that doesn't mean that everything else is on hold. We just released a range of improvements on the sign-up flow. It probably won't affect you (since you are already signed up), but it will improve the experience for your co-workers not already signed up (why not invite them to elmah.io?). Improvements I want to mention here are:

- Auto-fill of timezone (step 1)
- Show progress while working and general performance optimizations (step 2)
- More tutorials (Azure and Microsoft.Extensions.Logging) directly on the log setup page (step 3)
