There's a new integration in the [elmah.io App Store](https://elmah.io/features/appstore/): PagerDuty. PagerDuty is an incident platform supporting various integrations and notification methods like phone calls, SMS/text messages, and emails. With a few mouse clicks, setting up advanced notification rules for your team is easier than ever.

Check out the [PagerDuty documentation](https://docs.elmah.io/elmah-io-apps-pagerduty/) for details on how to set it up.
