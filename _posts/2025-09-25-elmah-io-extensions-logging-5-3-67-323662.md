We released a new version ([5.3.67](https://www.nuget.org/packages/Elmah.Io.Extensions.Logging/5.3.67)) of the `Elmah.Io.Extensions.Logging` package. The new version includes the following changes:

- Added support for setting the `Detail` field through log message properties.
- Newest `Elmah.Io.Client`.
- Made an `OnInstallation` hook available in options.
