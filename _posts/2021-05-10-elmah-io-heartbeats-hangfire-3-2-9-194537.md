We released a new version ([3.2.9](https://www.nuget.org/packages/Elmah.Io.Heartbeats.Hangfire/3.2.9)) of the `Elmah.Io.Heartbeats.Hangfire` package. The new version includes the following changes:

- Running on the newest `Elmah.Io.Client` package.
- The `Took` property is set to the time it took to execute the job.
- Documentation on the `ElmahIoHeartbeatAttribute` class.
