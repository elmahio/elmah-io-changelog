We keep expanding the Inspector tab with useful features and additional information, making it even easier to understand an exception. With the latest release, we added the following three features:

1. Link for known exception types to our exception landing pages available on elmah.io (like [System.NullReferenceException](https://elmah.io/exceptions/System.NullReferenceException/)).
2. A new popup on HResults showing the status, facility, and error code. There's also a link to hresult.info to dig even deeper into an HResult.
3. The values in the *Data* list have been made clickable for certain types as already known from the extended log message details. Quickly look up an IP address and more.
