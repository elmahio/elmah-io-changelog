Getting a continuous flow of new users is the only way that we can keep expanding elmah.io. Here is a list of actions for those of you who want to help spread the word:

[https://app.elmah.io/helpus/](https://app.elmah.io/helpus/)

Like you and me, potential users look at the various products out there, to find the one that fit their needs perfectly. We don't want to force users that don't fit with the feature set on elmah.io, but we do want to let people know that we exist. To spread knowledge about elmah.io, I could use your help.
