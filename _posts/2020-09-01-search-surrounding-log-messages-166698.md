When looking at an error logged to elmah.io, it sometimes helps to inspect log messages stored just before the error and maybe even after. With the new surround search feature, you can quickly search for the 15 log messages just before a specified message and the 5 log messages just after. Hover the log message and click the new surround icon to see surrounding log messages:
