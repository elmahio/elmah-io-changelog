The selected channel has been added to the Slack app settings page. You still need to re-install the app to pick another channel, but you can see which channel is already configured on existing apps.

![Channel on Slack app](/images/191151/214-73b470522cb227e928cfaf755ba286badb78e522.png)

To show the channel on already installed apps, you will need to re-install the app.
