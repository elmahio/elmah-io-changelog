We just launched one of the most requested features on elmah.io: the *Combined Daily Digest* email. The existing *Daily Digest* email sends one email per log. When you have multiple logs, getting a lot of emails may or may not be the optimal solution for getting an overview of the log activity since yesterday. With the new *Combined Daily Digest* you can bundle multiple logs in one email.

The way this works is by specifying on each log if you want it as an individual or combined email. The [Emails tab](https://app.elmah.io/profile/#emails) on your profile is extended with a new dropdown as shown here:

![Email configuration](/images/145673/490-1e0bd90bbb464333b31b3077ac9a4a98b2de6811.png)

If configured like in the screenshot above, you would get a *Daily Digest* email of the *Uptime.Checker* log and a *Combined Daily Digest* email of the *ErrorMail* and *EmailStatistics* logs.

All of your existing *Daily Digest* subscriptions are currently set as *Individual*. To combine them into a single email, go to [your profile](https://app.elmah.io/profile/#emails) and select *Combined*.

We would love to hear what you think.
