We have retired the elmah.io GPT-4 and elmah.io GPT-4 Turbo models from the ChatGPT app. The models are no longer supported by Microsoft Azure where our self-hosted models are deployed. Existing installations have been upgraded to GPT-4o. This change only applies to the models prefixed with 'elmah.io' available on the Enterprise plan. GPT-4 is still available on app installations using the OpenAI API. These models are prefixed with 'OpenAI' in the app:
