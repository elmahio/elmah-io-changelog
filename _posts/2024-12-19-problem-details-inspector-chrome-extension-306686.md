We published a (previously internal) Chrome extension named *Problem Details Inspector*. The extension better visualizes responses from endpoints returning [Problem Details](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc7807). While you can see these responses in the *Network* tab, you are looking at the raw JSON responses, and the requests and responses are mixed with other types. With *Problem Details Inspector* you will have an additional view showing only Problem Details responses and a nicely formatted view of the structured data.

If you are working with Problem Details, consider installing the extension from the Chrome Web Store here: [https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/problem-details-inspector/oclepcipaoebfiellkgpimicaglafpfm](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/problem-details-inspector/oclepcipaoebfiellkgpimicaglafpfm)
