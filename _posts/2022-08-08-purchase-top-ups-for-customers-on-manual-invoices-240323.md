Customers on manual invoices can now also purchase top-ups by inputting a credit card. This card will be used for any top-ups while the annual subscription will still be paid by whatever payment method agreed with each company.

![Purchase top-ups for customers on manual invoice](/images/240323/952-b9f22c1b22ef9994d59e99d64f33c93fc915e61c.png)

A benefit of this solution is that moving from payment using a manual invoice to a credit card can now be done without having to cancel the current subscription and create a new one. To hear about how to move from manual invoice to credit card, make sure to reach out to support.
