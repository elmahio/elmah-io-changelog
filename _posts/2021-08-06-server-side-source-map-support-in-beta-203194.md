Server-side JavaScript source map support has been launched in beta. In case you don't want to upload source maps alongside bundled and minified JavaScript files, you can integrate source map upload into your CI/CD pipeline or upload them manually through the elmah.io API. Check out the new [source map documentation](https://docs.elmah.io/sourcemaps/) for details. Server-side source map support is currently in beta and not recommended for production use.
