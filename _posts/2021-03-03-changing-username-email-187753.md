By popular demand, your username/email can now be changed directly from your [profile](https://app.elmah.io/profile/) (previously only possible when signing up with a social provider). There are a couple of limitations in terms of things that the feature cannot do like switching username to an existing user on elmah.io or switching when two-factor authentication is enabled.
