We released a new version ([5.1.76](https://www.nuget.org/packages/Elmah.Io.Client/5.1.76)) of the `Elmah.Io.Client` package. The new version includes the following change:

- Fixed a bug where using the constructor on the `Breadcrumb` class would throw away the values.

New minor versions of `Elmah.Io.AspNetCore`, `Elmah.Io.Extensions.Logging`, `Elmah.Io.Client.Extensions.SourceCode`, and `Elmah.Io.Client.Extensions.Correlation` will be released soon but won't receive individual changelogs since the only change is updating the dependency version.
