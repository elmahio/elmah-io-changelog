We released a new version ([3.0.44](https://www.nuget.org/packages/Elmah.Io.Cli/3.0.44)) of the `Elmah.Io.Cli` tool. The new version includes the following changes:

- Running on the newest `Elmah.Io.Client` package.
- Support for setting `correlationId` in the `log` command.
