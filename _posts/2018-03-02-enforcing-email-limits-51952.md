As mentioned previously, we have started working on enforcing the various limits on elmah.io. In April, message limits will be enforced. Today, we start enforcing the number of emails included in each plan (currently 4% of the included messages may generate an email). Pay attention to the usage graphs on the Subscription tab of your organization settings:

![Usage graphs](/images/51952/977-8b9663add4a4ad3585938a1ceb7672e506383693.png)

We are evaluating a number of different ways to notify you when limits are crossed. Getting ideas from you guys would be highly appreciated. How would you like to be notified? How often?
