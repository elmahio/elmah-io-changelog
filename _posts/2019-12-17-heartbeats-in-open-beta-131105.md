You may have heard about elmah.io Heartbeats already. We are ready to move Heartbeats to open beta. Heartbeats is for scheduled tasks and services as uptime monitoring is for APIs. With the built-in support for ASP.NET Core, Azure Functions, Hangfire, and everything else (using the API), Heartbeats makes sure that your scheduled tasks and services are actually running and running successfully. To learn more about Heartbeats we have a landing page available here:

https://elmah.io/features/heartbeats/

For help setting up Heartbeats there's a new section available on the documentation site:

https://docs.elmah.io/setup-heartbeats/

For now, a single Heartbeat has been added to all Business+ and Enterprise subscriptions. Those plans will get the Heartbeat feature once out of beta, but we are still discussing if we should put Heartbeats in more of the plans.

I'm really hoping for some feedback from you guys. We have been using Heartbeats internally on elmah.io for a couple of months now. We also had a closed beta where we received good feedback from elmah.io users.
