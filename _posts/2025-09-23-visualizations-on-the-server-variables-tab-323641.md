Let's be honest, looking at the keys and values on the *Server Variables* tab on the extended log message details is probably not the most exciting part of your day. But with the new visualizers on a range of values, we have tried to make it more helpful and visually appealing.

Emojis are used to represent different content types, byte values show a conversation to kb, mb, etc., user agents, status codes, and IPs are clickable, flags are shown for country codes, warnings are shown on outdated SSL/TLS versions, and more.
