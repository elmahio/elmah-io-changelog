We published a small redesign of message details:

![Redesigned message details](/images/169752/753-5bb728be2bb70908278be0589d3f8130ce651977.png)

The refine search buttons previously available when hovering a message, have been moved below the log message for easy access. The "action" buttons available in the black toolbar have been vertically aligned to make more room for longer log messages.
