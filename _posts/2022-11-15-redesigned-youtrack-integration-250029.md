We have built a new integration with YouTrack that replaces the old version which relied on a deprecated YouTrack API. The new app offers deeper integration with YouTrack where your projects are presented directly in the elmah.io UI. For anyone who has the app already installed, please go ahead and set it up again to make sure issues are synced with YouTrack going forward. Updated documentation is available here: [Install YouTrack App for elmah.io](https://docs.elmah.io/elmah-io-apps-youtrack/).
