We are currently testing a new version of app.elmah.io running on ASP.NET Core. If everything goes well, we will release it next week (March 9 and forward). Nothing visible is expected to change, but we have switched to the new authentication system available in ASP.NET Core. This means that you will need to log in again no matter if you have the *Stay signed in* toggle enabled or not. We are sorry for the inconvenience caused by this but we look forward to continuing our journey of improving the app.
