We're very happy to announce that elmah.io can now be integrated in your favorite AI client using the new [elmah.io MCP Server](https://mcp.elmah.io/). The service is still in beta, but we have been testing it with several users over the last few months. There are many benefits to having access to log data from your AI agents, such as quickly identifying errors and possibly having the client fix them directly in your codebase. We are looking forward to hearing what you are building.

The MCP server is available on `https://mcp.elmah.io/mcp` and requires the client to support an OAuth flow. There are examples on how to set this up in popular clients like Claude and CoPilot, both on the [MCP landing page](https://mcp.elmah.io/), as well as in the [elmah.io documentation](https://docs.elmah.io/setup-mcp-server/).
