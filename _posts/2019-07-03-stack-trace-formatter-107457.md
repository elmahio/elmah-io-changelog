New day - new tool 🛠 Today, I'm happy to introduce the elmah.io [Stack Trace Formatter](https://elmah.io/tools/stack-trace-formatter/). While stack traces are nicely formatted on elmah.io, I have seen too many unformatted stack traces on StackOverflow and on the web in general. With the new stack trace formatter, you can input a one-line stack trace and get a nicely formatted and syntax highlighted stack trace in return:

[![stack-trace-formatter.gif](/images/107457/986-46c9a893585c6b8adf2513d45ffd96695ac7c7b5.gif)](https://elmah.io/tools/stack-trace-formatter/)

With a single mouse-click, you can copy the formatted stack trace or download a screenshot for your blog post.
