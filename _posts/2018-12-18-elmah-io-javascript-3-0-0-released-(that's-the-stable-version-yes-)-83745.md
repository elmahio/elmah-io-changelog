We have finished beta testing our JavaScript integration, `elmah.io.javascript`. Today, the stable 3.0.0 is published to [npm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/elmah.io.javascript/v/3.0.0) and [NuGet](https://www.nuget.org/packages/elmah.io.javascript/3.0.0). In other words, JavaScript logging is available as a stable option. Maybe something to play with over Christmas? 🎅 [Documentation](https://docs.elmah.io/logging-to-elmah-io-from-javascript/) and [samples](https://github.com/elmahio/elmah.io.javascript/tree/master/samples) have been updated to reflect the recent changes.
