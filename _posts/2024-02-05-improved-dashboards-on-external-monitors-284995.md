If you are showing one of elmah.io's dashboards on an external monitor or want to do so, there are some improvements for you. Enabling a custom range, kiosk mode, and auto-refresh on all dashboards now updates the URL with these selected changes. This way, you can build the URL locally and copy it directly to the device outputting the content on the external screen. We also improved the dashboards by not showing the cookie popup, Intercom chat widget, etc. when kiosk mode is enabled.

In addition, there's a new help article to get you started: [How to show elmah.io dashboards on a big screen](https://docs.elmah.io/how-to-show-elmah-io-dashboards-on-a-big-screen/).
