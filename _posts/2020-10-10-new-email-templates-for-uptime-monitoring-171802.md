Logging when an endpoint is down and back up uses your existing error logs and email configuration. This means that logging errors from your applications and up/down messages from Uptime Monitoring always shared an email template. With a recent addition, Uptime Monitoring now has its own set of email templates that (hopefully) visualize the current situation better and leave out all of the information not applicable to uptime errors:
