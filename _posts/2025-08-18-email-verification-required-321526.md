We have tightened security on elmah.io by now requiring email verification when signing up new users. This applies to both users signing up with a username/password and through a social provider. The change doesn't require any immediate actions from existing users. New users will need to verify the email address used when signing up through the normal flow or an invite.

We are working on extending user administration with an overview of verified and non-verified users to help organization administrators get all email addresses in the organization verified, if this is desired.
