You may know about the free tools we have on the elmah.io website like the [Web.config Validator](https://elmah.io/tools/configvalidator/) and the [Stack Trace Formatter](https://elmah.io/tools/stack-trace-formatter/). As suggested in the name, the Stack Trace Formatter converts unformatted .NET stack traces to a pretty syntax-highlighted output that you can save as an image or copy for use on StackOverflow, your blog, etc.

We have extended the Stack Trace Formatter with a new *Share* button that will let you share a formatted stack trace as a link with a friend or colleague.
