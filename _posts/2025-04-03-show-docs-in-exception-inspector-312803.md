The Exception Inspector has been extended with a new *Show Docs* button on known exceptions. When clicking the button, an overlay with parts of the exception documentation from Microsoft Learn is shown directly in the inspector. This gives a quick overview of the logged exception, as well as helpful remarks about how to avoid the exception in the future:
