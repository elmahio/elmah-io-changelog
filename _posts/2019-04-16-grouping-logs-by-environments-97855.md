I'm very happy to tell you about the latest addition to elmah.io: Environments (at least that is the name for now). Being able to group similar error logs together, is the most requested feature on elmah.io. While both re-ordering and coloring logs have been available for years, you guys have requested better grouping on the dashboard (as well as in the left menu). With Environments, you can finally select one of four predefined environments to group logs by:

![Environments](/images/97855/369-125ab615f2700e2c3a25d5b1ea9859d2adbfd752.gif)

I know that some of you are already asking: "What if I want to group my logs by customers, service, or something third?". We've picked environments for v1 of this feature since that is the most frequent request. If you are looking for another way of grouping your logs, please get in contact and let's have a chat.
