We have released several improvements to Bugster. The biggest change is that Bugster can now answer simple questions about your log data. When on the Log Search page, you can ask questions like "What are the 3 recent errors in my log?" and "When is the most frequent error?". Asking log-specific questions requires *Extended Access* to be enabled, as explained in the [Bugster help article](https://docs.elmah.io/how-to-configure-and-use-bugster/).

You can still click the *Analyze with Bugster* button on a specific error to get Bugster's help with fixing the error. As a new thing, both this mode as well as the Bugster chat available on docs.elmah.io now offer the possibility of asking follow-up questions.

We have multiple improvements planned already, but feel free to share your experiences and ideas using the chat widget in the lower right corner.
