We have started bringing elmah.io to mobile platforms running on .NET. We currently released support for [Xamarin](https://docs.elmah.io/logging-to-elmah-io-from-xamarin/) and [Uno](https://docs.elmah.io/logging-to-elmah-io-from-uno/). Support for MAUI will be included as well when .NET 6 is released.

We are interested in hearing from users who would like to try out the new packages. Don't hesitate to reach out through support if you have questions and feedback.
