After the official release of Blazor WebAssembly yesterday, we resumed our experiment with creating an integration to elmah.io. Blazor WebAssembly is still very limited in supporting third-party libraries. But for those of you who like to play, we wrote a new section about how to [log to elmah.io from Blazor WebAssembly Apps](https://docs.elmah.io/logging-to-elmah-io-from-blazor/#blazor-webassembly-app-wasm). Please notice that the code is highly experimental.
