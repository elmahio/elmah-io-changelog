You may remember the (rather unhandy) dropdown to manage log access on a user on the organization settings page. This has been replaced with a slide-in to make it more consistent with the rest of the elmah.io UI. As part of the move, we also added environments to the list of logs to better support multiple logs with the same name in different environments:
