To reduce the risk of someone getting access to your log data, we have integrated with Have I Been Pwned. This means that can no longer sign up for elmah.io, or change the password on an existing user, with a password appearing in one or more data breaches. We recommend you to use a password manager like LastPass or 1password to generate a unique and complex password for your elmah.io account.
