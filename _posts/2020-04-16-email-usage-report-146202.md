There's a new email usage report available on the *Subscription* tab on the organization settings page. When clicking the info icon next to *Emails* you get an overview of how you've spent your monthly email quota so far:

![Email quota](/images/146202/670-17c8121489001286584a6e4f85faa8e34d548674.png)

We only started grouping the statistics by email a couple of days ago, why you may see a row titled "Unknown" this month. From next month, all emails will be correctly grouped by email addresses.
