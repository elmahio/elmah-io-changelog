We have enabled DNSSEC on the elmah.io domain. DNSSEC is a protocol that adds a layer of trust and authentication to DNS by using digital signatures based on public key cryptography. We have been promised by Cloudflare that the change should not affect anything else than improving elmah.io's security. Let us know if you experience anything that could be related to this.
