We fixed an issue where users needed to sign back in every time we deployed. If you haven't been logged in since yesterday, you will need to sign in on your next visit. Users are still logged out after 1 hour of inactivity unless the *Stay signed in* toggle is enabled on the profile. Sorry for the inconvenience.
