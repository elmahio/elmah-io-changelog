We have included three new models for Bugster: GPT 5.4, GPT 5.4 mini, and Gemini 3 Flash. That's right, you can now run Bugster on Gemini as well. When picking Gemini or one of the OpenAI-hosted models, you still need to provide your own API key. Both GPT-5.4 and GPT-5.4 mini are available as elmah.io-hosted for Enterprise users as well.

While adding the new models, we have deprecated all GPT-4.1* models. If you have one of these models configured, please upgrade to a corresponding v5 model. We will remove all support for 4.1 models on Oct 14, 2026 (or before if everyone has moved).
