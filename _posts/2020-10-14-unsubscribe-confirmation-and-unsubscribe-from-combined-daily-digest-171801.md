We pushed some improvements to the unsubscribe links available in the footer of all transactional emails we send out. All unsubscribe links now show a confirmation step. This will make sure that you don't unsubscribe by mistake and avoid unsubscribing unintentionally if the browser makes a prefetch-get request to the unsubscribe page.
