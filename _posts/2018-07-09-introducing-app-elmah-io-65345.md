We have just rolled out a new version of elmah.io. Going forward, https://elmah.io will contain all the public pages like the front page, features page and more. When you want to browse through your error logs, you click a new login button in the top right, which will redirect your browser to the new subdomain: https://app.elmah.io. app.elmah.io contains all the features, that you already use, like searching your logs, paying for your subscription and more. We tried defining as many redirect rules as possible, but you may need to update a few bookmarks. Since this is a new subdomain, you need to log in again.

With this change, we create a clear distinction between our product information pages (for sales) and the actual app. This makes it a lot easier for us to make changes to one, without having to worry about breaking the other.
