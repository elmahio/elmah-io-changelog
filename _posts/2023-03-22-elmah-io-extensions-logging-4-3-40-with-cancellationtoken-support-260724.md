We released a new version ([4.3.40](https://www.nuget.org/packages/Elmah.Io.Extensions.Logging/4.3.40)) of the `Elmah.Io.Extensions.Logging` NuGet package. The new version includes the following changes:

- `CancellationToken` support.
- New sample for Isolated Azure Functions.
- Running on the newest version of `Elmah.Io.Client`.
