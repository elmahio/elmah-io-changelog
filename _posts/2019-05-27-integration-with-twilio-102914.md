Ever wanted SMS/Text messages when new errors are logged? With the new Integration with Twilio you can. After signing up for a pay-as-you-go subscription at Twilio, telling elmah.io to send messages requires nothing more than installing an app. Check out [Install Twilio for elmah.io](https://docs.elmah.io/elmah-io-apps-twilio/) for more details.
