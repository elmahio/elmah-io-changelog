Bugster, our helpful bot, has moved into app.elmah.io too. You may already know Bugster from the documentation site. If not, it can answer questions about elmah.io from sources like our documentation. It is still training so look at it as a beta for now.

![image.png](/images/311827/465-204969652758999bb2552af4805556cb17dae676.png)

*Disclaimer: Bugster is in no way a replacement for the chat and email support which is still available and works just as you know it. Also, no personal data or log data are shared with Bugster (unless you manually share it through a question, of course). Bugster doesn't have access to anything but its general model which is built on top of docs.elmah.io. Bugster uses a self-hosted LLM on Azure to avoid your questions being used for training by OpenAI and similar.*
