Purchasing top-ups have been available on elmah.io for a couple of years. But, if you wanted to purchase more than one, you would need to make multiple purchases and in result get multiple invoices for your accountant to worry about. With the new top-ups dialog, you can now purchase multiple top-ups in one go:
