Microsoft no longer seems to support links in the headline in Teams messages, why we added a new *View* button in all messages. Clicking the *View* button will take you directly to the logged messages on elmah.io. To get the new app, please re-install the Microsoft Teams app on the log settings page.

![image.png](/images/199792/464-e08956378be11761a9a14d7f4ee97ac948603b32.png)

Microsoft recently changed something around the webhook URLs generated by integrations like the one with elmah.io. If you are having trouble getting messages through to Teams, please check out this [Troubleshooting](https://docs.elmah.io/elmah-io-apps-teams/#troubleshooting) document for help.
