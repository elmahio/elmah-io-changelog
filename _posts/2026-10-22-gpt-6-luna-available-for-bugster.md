GPT-6 Luna is now available for Bugster as both a model hosted by OpenAI and by us. The elmah.io-hosted option is available for Enterprise users. When picking the OpenAI-hosted model, you still need to provide your own API key. You will find the configuration for Bugster on the Organization Settings page.
