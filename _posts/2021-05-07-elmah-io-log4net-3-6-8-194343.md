We released a new version ([3.6.8](https://www.nuget.org/packages/Elmah.Io.Log4Net/3.6.8)) of the `Elmah.Io.Log4Net` package. The new version includes the following changes:

- Running on the newest `Elmah.Io.Client` package.
- Support for setting correlation id from properties.
