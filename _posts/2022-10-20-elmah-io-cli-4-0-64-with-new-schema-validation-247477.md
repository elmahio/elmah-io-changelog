We released a new version ([4.0.64](https://www.nuget.org/packages/Elmah.Io.Cli/4.0.64)) of the elmah.io CLI. The new version includes the following changes:

- Diagnose command now validates `nlog.config` and `log4net.config` files against their XML schemas.
- Running on the newest version of `Spectre.Console`.
