We released a new version ([4.1.18](https://www.nuget.org/packages/Elmah.Io.Log4Net/4.1.18)) of the `Elmah.Io.Log4Net` package. The new version includes the following changes:

- Running on the newest version of the `Elmah.Io.Client` package.
- Include readme in the NuGet package.
- Building on .NET 6 and new .NET 6 samples.

Just for the record, neither log4net nor `Elmah.Io.Log4Net` is affected by the Log4j vulnerability issue.
