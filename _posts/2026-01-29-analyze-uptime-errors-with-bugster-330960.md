Bugster can now help analyze failing uptime monitoring checks. As on other errors, you can click the *Analyze with Bugster* button on the error details. When analyzing uptime errors, Bugster automatically fetches results from the selected regions to help you determine why an endpoint fails and what you can do to resolve the issue.
