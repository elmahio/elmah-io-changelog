Integrating your error logs with Microsoft Teams is easier than ever. We just published an elmah.io app to the Microsoft Teams store, that will guide you through the steps of showing new errors in a Teams channel of your choice.

Check out the updated documentation for information about how to get started: [Install Microsoft Teams App for elmah.io](https://docs.elmah.io/elmah-io-apps-teams/).
