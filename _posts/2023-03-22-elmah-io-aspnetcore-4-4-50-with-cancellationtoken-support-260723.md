We released a new version ([4.4.50](https://www.nuget.org/packages/Elmah.Io.AspNetCore/4.4.50)) of the `Elmah.Io.AspNetCore` NuGet package. The new version includes the following changes:

- `CancellationToken` support.
- Running on the newest version of `Elmah.Io.Client`.
