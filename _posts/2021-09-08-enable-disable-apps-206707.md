Apps can now be enabled and disabled directly from the Apps page. This way, you no longer need to remove an app to temporarily disable it.

![enable-disable-app.gif](/images/206707/664-1615b90369ae09b8fddb7cfaf3ce313ed28aee81.gif)

In some cases, we will start disabling misconfigured apps. When doing so, an error message will be shown on the app with instructions on what to fix before enabling the app.
